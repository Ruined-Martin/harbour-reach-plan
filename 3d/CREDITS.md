Only floors, paving, deck and lawn carry maps. Walls, ceilings, fabrics and furniture are flat colours with a roughness value; no roughness maps ship. The wood, tile and stone relief is a gentle normal map; the 1024 px files load only on the `high` tier. The sets for walnut, plaster and fabric, and the 1024 px concrete and grass files, were removed in the F4 balance round.
